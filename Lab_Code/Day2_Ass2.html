Motor Vehicles in the Indian Market

Scooter
It is known for its ease of use and fuel efficiency.

Scooters are lightweight, two-wheeled vehicles with a step-through frame and a platform for the rider's feet. They are popular for short-distance commuting and urban transportation.

Motorcycle

Motorcycles are two-wheeled vehicles that are powered by an engine. They come in various styles, including sport bikes, cruisers, and touring bikes. Motorcycles are known for their speed, agility, and fuel efficiency.

Electric Vehicle

Electric vehicles (EVs) are powered by electric motors and use energy stored in rechargeable batteries. They produce zero emissions and are considered environmentally friendly. EVs are gaining popularity in India due to government incentives and increasing awareness of environmental issues.
